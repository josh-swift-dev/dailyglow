DailyGlow Support

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Thanks for using DailyGlow! This page provides help with reminders, notifications, DailyGlow Pro, purchases, and other common questions.

DailyGlow is free to get started.

Create and manage up to 5 reminders for free. If you need more, DailyGlow Pro provides a lifetime upgrade with additional features. There is no subscription.

What is included for free?

The free version of DailyGlow allows you to create and manage up to 5 reminders with basic daily and one-time scheduling.

Free users can also use core features such as My Day, completion tracking, local notifications, and supported widget features.

What does DailyGlow Pro unlock?

DailyGlow Pro unlocks:

  • Unlimited reminders
  • Repeating and interval-based reminders
  • Weekday schedules
  • Weekend schedules
  • Custom-day schedules
  • Calendar integration

Is DailyGlow Pro a subscription?

No. DailyGlow Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Once purchased, there is no recurring DailyGlow Pro subscription fee.

How do I purchase DailyGlow Pro?

Open DailyGlow and go to the Settings tab. Find the DailyGlow Pro section and select the lifetime unlock option.

The purchase is completed securely through Apple's App Store purchase system.

I already purchased Pro. Why is it locked?

Open DailyGlow, go to Settings, and select Restore Purchases.

Make sure the device is using the Apple ID associated with the original purchase.

DailyGlow uses RevenueCat to verify the purchase and restore your DailyGlow Pro entitlement.

Will restoring charge me again?

Restore Purchases is intended to check for a previous eligible purchase. It does not initiate a new DailyGlow Pro purchase.

What happens to my reminders if I reinstall the app?

DailyGlow reminder information is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove locally stored reminder data. Restoring DailyGlow Pro restores the purchase entitlement; it does not restore reminder content that was deleted from the device.

Notifications are not appearing

Check that notifications are enabled for DailyGlow in your iPhone Settings. Device Focus modes and other notification settings may also affect whether alerts are displayed.

Calendar integration is not working

Calendar integration is a DailyGlow Pro feature. Make sure DailyGlow Pro is unlocked and that DailyGlow has permission to access your Calendar.

I reached the 5-reminder limit

The free version supports up to 5 saved reminders. Existing reminders can continue to be used. To create additional reminders, unlock DailyGlow Pro from Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. DailyGlow does not require a DailyGlow account or login.

Does DailyGlow contain ads?

No. DailyGlow does not contain third-party advertising.
