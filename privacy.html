DailyGlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

DailyGlow is designed to provide reminders and routine tracking while keeping your personal reminder information private. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use DailyGlow.

Information You Create in DailyGlow

Reminder information you create in DailyGlow, including reminder titles, notes, schedules, completion information, categories, preferences, and related routine information, is stored locally on your device.

DailyGlow does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the app.

Notifications

DailyGlow may request permission to send local notifications. These notifications are generated using the reminder schedules you create in the app.

You can disable notification access at any time through your device's Settings.

Calendar Access

DailyGlow Pro includes optional Calendar integration. If you choose to use this feature, DailyGlow may request permission to create or manage Calendar events related to your reminders.

Calendar access is optional and is only used when you choose to enable Calendar integration.

In-App Purchases

DailyGlow offers an optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase called DailyGlow Pro.

The free version of DailyGlow supports up to 5 reminders with basic daily and one-time scheduling. DailyGlow Pro unlocks additional features including unlimited reminders, advanced repeating schedules, custom scheduling options, and Calendar integration.

DailyGlow Pro is a lifetime unlock and is not a subscription.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. DailyGlow does not receive or store your credit card number, banking information, or other payment credentials.

RevenueCat

DailyGlow uses RevenueCat to help manage and verify in-app purchase entitlements.

RevenueCat may process information related to purchases and app usage necessary to determine whether DailyGlow Pro has been purchased or restored. This may include an anonymous app user identifier, transaction or receipt information, product information, device or operating system information, and purchase status.

DailyGlow does not use RevenueCat to collect your reminder titles, reminder notes, schedules, or other reminder content.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at: RevenueCat Privacy Policy .

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased DailyGlow Pro using your Apple ID, you can use the Restore Purchases option within DailyGlow to attempt to restore access on a compatible device.

Advertising

DailyGlow does not contain third-party advertising.

Tracking

DailyGlow does not use your reminder content for advertising or sell your reminder information to advertisers.

Children's Privacy

DailyGlow does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account registration system because the app does not require user accounts.

Data Security

DailyGlow is designed to minimize the amount of information transmitted outside your device. Reminder and routine content is stored locally on your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DailyGlow features, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
